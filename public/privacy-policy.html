Privacy Policy

What we collect

To use SmartWaste, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) a residential address and profile picture. When you submit a report, we collect the photo, waste type, description, and the GPS location you provide.

How we use it

Your information is used to operate your account, let local authorities respond to reports, show your own report history, and send you in-app notifications about your reports' status.

Who can see your data

Report locations and photos are visible to Admin users so they can respond. Your name may be shown alongside a report unless you submit it anonymously.

Data storage

Data is stored securely on the application server. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before saving.

Your rights

You can update your profile information at any time, and you can contact an administrator to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project administrator.
