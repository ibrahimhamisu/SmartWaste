SmartWaste

Smart Reporting. Cleaner Communities.

What is SmartWaste?

SmartWaste is a Smart Community Waste Management and Illegal Dumping Reporting System. It lets residents report waste issues and illegal dumping with a photo and location, and lets local authorities track, manage, and resolve those reports from a central dashboard.

Why it matters

Illegal dumping and poor waste tracking harm public health and the environment, and residents often have no simple way to report problems or know if anything is being done. SmartWaste closes that gap with a fast reporting flow and transparent status tracking.
